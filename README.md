# 📈 DataPulse

**Your whole business, one clear picture.**

DataPulse is a simple home for your business numbers. Add your files — sales, expenses, anything — and it turns them into clear charts and big numbers you can actually understand. No training needed, no confusing screens. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use DataPulse.

## What it does

- **Boards** — your own wall of charts and big numbers. Arrange it however you like, like pinning things on a softboard.
- **Analytics** — one page that shows how everything is going: money coming in, money going out, and what needs a push. All in pictures.
- **Invoices** — make proper GST bills in a minute. Fill in what you sold, and the tax math, the totals, even the amount in words — all done for you. Print it or save it as a PDF and send it.
- **GST summary** — at month-end, the numbers your accountant asks for are already ready: tax collected, month by month, ready to hand over.
- **Your papers, always with you** — GST certificate, PAN, bank statements, contracts — keep photos and files of all your important papers in one safe place, and open them any time.
- **Add data your way** — upload a spreadsheet and it reads the columns for you, paste a link from Google Sheets, or just type the numbers in directly — it works like a mini Excel.
- **Share with your team** — let a partner or accountant see a board. Or send a link anyone can open, no sign-in needed.
- **Reports on autopilot** — pick a day, and a fresh report lands in your email every week or month. It comes to you.
- **Goals** — set a monthly target and watch the bar fill up as the month goes on. Cross it and it celebrates with you. 🎉
- **Works on your phone** — check your numbers from anywhere, and you can even install it on your home screen like an app.
- **हिंदी में भी** — switch the app to Hindi with one tap.

## 💬 A few things worth knowing

- **Your data is yours alone.** Everything you add is private to your account. No one else can see it.
- **Amounts are in rupees**, shown the Indian way — lakh and crore.
- **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot?" on the sign-in screen and a reset link comes to your email.

## 🛠 Want to run your own copy?

It's free. You need two things: a home for the app (Netlify hosts it free) and a free account at [supabase.com](https://supabase.com) to keep the data safe. The short version:

1. Create a free project at supabase.com
2. Connect the two (the app asks for two keys from the Supabase settings — paste them in)
3. Press the setup buttons, and it's ready

The detailed steps live in the project files for anyone comfortable with code. Everyone else: just use the live link at the top. 🙂
